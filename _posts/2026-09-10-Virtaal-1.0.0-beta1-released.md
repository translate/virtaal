---
title: Virtaal 1.0.0-beta1 Released
category: releases
---

Virtaal is back after being dormant since 2020, ported from Python 2/GTK2
to Python 3/GTK3 - a rewrite of most of the application's internals with
no intended change to the day-to-day translating experience.

This is a beta - see the full [release notes](https://docs.translatehouse.org/projects/virtaal/en/latest/releases/1.0.0.html)
for highlights and known issues.

Windows and macOS binaries are currently unsigned - Gatekeeper/SmartScreen
will warn on first launch.
