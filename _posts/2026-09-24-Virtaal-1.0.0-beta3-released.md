The Python 3/GTK3 port continues - see the full
[release notes](https://docs.translatehouse.org/projects/virtaal/en/latest/releases/1.0.0.html)
for highlights and known issues.

Windows and macOS binaries are currently unsigned - Gatekeeper/SmartScreen
will warn on first launch:

- **macOS**: right-click (or Control-click) `Virtaal.app` in Finder and
  choose **Open** - the dialog that appears this time has an **Open**
  button. Only needed once. If it instead says the app is damaged, run
  `xattr -cr /path/to/Virtaal.app` in Terminal first.
- **Windows**: click **More info**, then **Run anyway** on the
  "Windows protected your PC" screen.
