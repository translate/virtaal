---
title: Virtaal 1.0.0-rc2 Released
category: releases
---

The second release candidate for Virtaal 1.0.0. It runs on macOS 15 or
later (rc1 needed macOS 26), adds a Flatpak for Linux, fixes the problems
testers found in rc1, and brings new and updated translations. See the
[release page](https://github.com/translate/virtaal/releases/tag/v1.0.0-rc2)
for what's new since rc1, and please
[report anything that goes wrong](https://github.com/translate/virtaal/issues).

The interface text is still frozen for 1.0.0. Translations sent in by
21 November ship in 1.0.0: [Translate Virtaal](/translate.html) shows how
far each language has got and how to send one in.
