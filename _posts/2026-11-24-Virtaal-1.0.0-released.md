---
title: Virtaal 1.0.0 Released
category: releases
---

Virtaal 1.0.0 is out: the first stable release since 0.7.1, rebuilt on
Python 3 and GTK 3, with signed builds for Windows and macOS and a
Flatpak for Linux. Thank you to everyone who tested the release
candidates and to the translators who brought its interface up to date.
See the [release page](https://github.com/translate/virtaal/releases/tag/v1.0.0)
for what's in it, and please
[report anything that goes wrong](https://github.com/translate/virtaal/issues).

Translations sent in now go into the next release:
[Translate Virtaal](/translate.html) shows how far each language has got
and how to send one in.
